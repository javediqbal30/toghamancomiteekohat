# Part 1: Constitution and Rules of Procedure

## Preamble

The Togh Public Committee will be a non-political, non-sectarian public welfare platform. Its main aims are:

- peace and order in the area
- respect for the law
- protection of public rights
- transparent development
- opportunities for young people
- protection of common (shared) lands
- bringing public problems to the right departments in a legal and peaceful way

The Committee will not be used for the personal benefit of any person, family, or group. The **common good of the whole area** will always come first.

---

## Chapter 1 — Basic Objectives

**Article 1: Peace and Order**
Promote peace, brotherhood, mutual respect, and unity in the area. Play a positive role in solving disputes peacefully.

**Article 2: Preventing Crime and Social Evils**
Work with the relevant departments, in a legal and peaceful way, against drug dealing, crime, gambling, and other illegal or socially harmful activities.

**Article 3: Rights of Children and Women**
Work to protect the legal, educational, social, and basic rights of children and women.

**Article 4: Development Work**
Bring public problems about roads, water, electricity, education, health, cleanliness, sports, and other basic facilities to the notice of the relevant departments.

**Article 5: Royalty and Development Funds**
Collect information and contact the relevant departments so that royalty, development funds, and other public resources of the area are used in a transparent, fair, and legal way.

**Article 6: Protection of Shamilat (Common Lands)**
Approach the relevant departments to take legal action against illegal occupation, sale or purchase, or wrongful use of the area's common and shared lands.

**Article 7: Opportunities for Youth**
Work to promote jobs, technical training, education, business opportunities, and sports facilities for young people.

**Article 8: Patwari and Revenue System**
Contact the relevant authorities to make sure revenue records are correct and clear, and that people get services on time.

**Article 9: Education and Health**
Make a joint effort to improve educational institutions and basic health facilities in the area.

**Article 10: Environment and Natural Resources**
Spread awareness and promote practical steps to protect water, land, forests, pastures, and other natural resources.

---

## Chapter 2 — Structure of the Committee

The Committee will have the following structure.

**Advisors**
- Advisor group:
  - Legal Advisors

**Executive Committee**
- Patron / Chairman
- President
- Senior Vice President
- Second Vice President
- General Secretary
- President for Youth Affairs
- President for Overseas Affairs
- Secretary for Overseas Affairs
- Joint Secretary
- Second Joint Secretary
- Finance Secretary
- Information and Media Secretary

**Other bodies**
- Related departmental / sub-committees
- Ulama (religious scholars)
- Women's Wing
- General Body

*More positions can be created when needed, with the approval of the General Body.*

---

## Chapter 3 — General Body

**1. Role of the General Body**
The General Body is the biggest collective forum of the Committee.

**2. Powers**
The General Body will:
- Approve the basic policy.
- Elect the cabinet and can also dissolve it, by its vote.
- Review the yearly performance.
- Review all matters under the guidance of the Executive Body.
- Elect the office holders.
- Approve any change to the constitution.

---

## Chapter 4 — Executive Committee

The Executive Committee is responsible for daily work and for carrying out the decisions approved by the General Body.

**Main responsibilities:**
- Carry out approved plans
- Keep contact with government departments
- Approve important resolutions
- Review public complaints
- Monitor development work
- Monitor financial matters
- Review financial reports
- Hold meetings
- Prepare progress reports

---

## Chapter 5 — Duties of Office Holders

### President
The President will:
- Represent the Committee.
- Chair the meetings.
- Protect the basic principles of the organization.
- Guide the Committee in important matters.

**The President does not have the power to make important decisions of the organization alone.**

### Vice President
The Vice President will:
- Take over the duties when the President is absent.
- Stay in contact with the President and the General Secretary.
- Help in watching over the Committee's projects.

### General Secretary
This post will be very important for administration. The General Secretary will:
- Call regular meetings.
- Prepare the agenda for meetings.
- Have the Minutes of Meeting prepared.
- Keep the record of resolutions.
- Watch over the carrying out of approved decisions.
- Write letters to government departments and handle correspondence.
- Prepare Progress Reports of projects.

**Key rule:** Regular meetings are called by the General Secretary or with his approval, except in emergencies.

### Joint Secretary
The Joint Secretary will:
- Help the General Secretary.
- Help look after records and documents.
- Take over the General Secretary's duties when he is absent.

### Finance Secretary
The Finance Secretary will:
- Keep a record of all income and expenses.
- Give a receipt for every donation.
- Keep vouchers for all expenses.
- Present regular financial reports.
- Not use funds without the Committee's approval.

**Basic rule:** No money will be received or spent without a record and a receipt.

### Powers of Advisors and Legal Advisors
Office holders must consult the Advisors and Legal Advisors in all matters and decisions, so that any legal problem can be checked.

### President for Youth Affairs
Review matters connected with young people and contact with them.

### Role of Ulama (Religious Scholars)
Review all resolutions and decisions from the point of view of Shariah (Islamic law).

### President and Secretary for Overseas Affairs
Keep in touch with overseas Pakistanis from this area and play an important role in contacts and financial support.

---

## Chapter 6 — Procedure for Meetings

1. **Regular meetings:** The Committee will hold regular meetings according to a fixed schedule.
2. **Agenda:** An agenda of important topics will be prepared before the meeting.
3. **Minutes of Meeting:** The proceedings of every meeting will be written down.
4. **Resolutions:** Important decisions will be made through a proper resolution.
5. **Quorum:** A fixed number of members must be present for a meeting to be valid.
   *Proposal:* At least 50% + 1 of all Executive members present will count as a quorum.
6. **Voting:** If there is no agreement on a matter, voting will be held in the fixed way.

---

## Chapter 7 — Resolution System

Every resolution must have at least this information:

- Resolution number
- Date
- Subject
- Short description of the problem
- Decision
- Responsible person / committee
- Last date for completion
- Resources needed
- Follow-up date

**Example:**

> **Resolution No. 05/2026**
> **Subject:** Water supply in the area
> **Decision:** An application will be given to the relevant department.
> **Responsible:** General Secretary
> **Deadline:** 15 October 2026
> **Follow-up:** A report will be presented in the next meeting.

---

## Chapter 8 — Financial Transparency

The following rules apply to the Committee's money matters:

1. Keep a record of all income.
2. Keep a record of all expenses.
3. Receipts are compulsory.
4. Vouchers for expenses.
5. If possible, do transactions through a bank account.
6. A person's personal account must not be used as the permanent Committee account.
7. Monthly or quarterly financial report.
8. Yearly review of accounts.
9. Prior approval of the Committee for big expenses.

---

## Chapter 9 — Conflict of Interest

If a member has a personal or family interest in any project, contract, land, purchase, or money matter, he must tell the Committee about it.

If needed, he must stay out of the vote on that decision.

---

## Chapter 10 — Complaint System

The Committee will keep a regular **Complaint Register**.

For every complaint, the following will be recorded:

**Complaint No. → Date → Complaint → Evidence → Relevant department → Responsible member → Action → Current status**

No campaign of public blame or defamation will be run against anyone without proof.

---

## Chapter 11 — Respect for the Law

The Committee:
- will not take the law into its own hands.
- will not threaten anyone.
- will not use force or violence.
- will not damage government or private property.
- will not take action against anyone in an illegal way.

The Committee's role will be **lawful, peaceful, public, and representative**.

---

## Chapter 12 — Political Neutrality

Because the Committee is a non-political body:

- It will not be a branch of any political party.
- Its funds will not be used for political activities.
- Its name will not be used for any candidate's election campaign.
- Every person with a political affiliation will get equal respect as an individual, under the rules of the organization.

---

## Chapter 13 — Media and Social Media

Only an **authorized spokesperson** will issue official statements on behalf of the Committee.

The following will not be published in the Committee's name:
- Unverified news
- Rumors
- Personal accusations
- Character assassination
- Provocative (inflammatory) material

---

## Chapter 14 — Project System

For every project, this method will be followed:

**Problem → Proposal → Resolution → Responsible Person → Budget → Timeline → Progress → Completion Report**

This will keep it clear **who will do what work, when, and under which decision.**

---

## Chapter 15 — Accountability

Every office holder is answerable for his position.

If a member:
- misuses funds,
- misuses powers,
- uses the organization's name for personal gain, or
- does not do his duties regularly,

he will be given a chance to explain, and then suitable action can be taken under the constitution.

---

## Chapter 16 — Difference of Opinion

Difference of opinion will be seen as part of making the organization better.

Every member has the right to give his opinion. But once the final decision is made through the fixed procedure, all members will respect it.

---

## Chapter 17 — Term of Office

The term of office holders can be fixed, for example, at **2 years**.

When the term ends:
- Review of performance
- New nominations / elections
- Handing over of duties
- Handing over of financial and administrative records

---

## Chapter 18 — Amendments to the Constitution

No basic change will be made to this constitution at the wish of one person.

For an amendment, these steps are needed:

1. Written proposal
2. Discussion in a meeting
3. Proper resolution
4. Approval by the fixed majority
5. Entry in the record

---

## The Most Important Principle

> **"The Togh Public Committee is not the property of any person. It is a trust of the people."**

All office holders and members of this Committee are not owners of power. They are **representatives who are answerable to the people.**

Every work of the Committee will follow the principles of **law, transparency, merit, joint consultation, written proof, and public interest.**

# Part 2: Policy of the Togh Aman Committee

**Principle of Written Resolution**
Every important decision should be made through a proper written resolution. A verbal decision should not be treated as the final decision of the organization.

**Proper Way to Call Meetings**
Meetings should be called by the General Secretary or an authorized office holder, with a proper notice and agenda. A suitable procedure should also be set for emergency meetings.

**Agenda and Written Record**
An agenda should be prepared before every meeting. Minutes of Meeting (MoM) should be prepared after it, clearly showing decisions, responsible persons, and deadlines.

**Majority and Joint Decision-Making**
Important decisions should not be made by one person's opinion or power. They should be made through the Committee's joint consultation and the fixed procedure.

**Full Transparency in Money Matters**
All financial matters of the organization should be kept safe through proper records, receipts, vouchers, and bank/cash records.

**Proper Record of Donations**
A proper receipt should be given for every donation, gift, or financial help. A full account of income and expenses should be kept.

**Approval for Use of Funds**
Organization funds should not be spent at the personal will of any one person. For spending above a fixed limit, the Committee's approval is needed.

**Financial Report**
A financial report should be presented to the Committee monthly, quarterly, or yearly, so that everyone knows about income and expenses.

**Responsibility and Deadline for Projects**
For every project, one responsible person/committee should be appointed, and a Target Date and clear expected results should be set.

**Progress Report System**
A monthly or quarterly progress report should be presented for every project, showing what is completed, in progress, and delayed.

**Avoiding Conflict of Interest**
If an office holder or member has a personal, family, or business interest in a decision or project, he should declare it and, if needed, step away from that decision.

**Proof Instead of Accusation**
Before accusing any person, department, or government official, the facts, documents, and reliable information should be checked.

**Proper Complaint System**
A proper Complaint Register/System should be created for public complaints. Every complaint should be recorded with a number, date, and responsible person, and its progress should be tracked.

**Privacy and Protection of Personal Information**
Complaints, personal information, and sensitive documents should not be made public without need, and should be kept limited to authorized people.

**Lawful Cooperation with Government Departments**
The organization will not replace law enforcement, revenue, police, administration, or other government departments. It will help bring public problems to the right forum in a legal and peaceful way.

**Principle of Not Taking the Law into One's Hands**
No member of the organization will take the law into his own hands, threaten anyone, use force, or damage any government or private property.

**Neutrality**
The organization will not be used for the personal benefit of any person, family, community, or group. All decisions will follow the common good of the area and the fixed principles.

**Positions Are Responsibility, Not Personal Power**
No position in the organization will be a source of personal power or privileges. It will be seen as a responsibility before the people and the organization.

**Clear Division of Powers**
The powers and duties of the President, General Secretary, Finance Secretary, and other office holders should be made clear in writing, so that powers are not misused.

**Proper Record of Documents**
All resolutions, meeting proceedings, financial records, government letters, applications, replies, and project reports should be kept safe.

**Yearly Performance Review**
At the end of the year, there should be a proper review of the organization's performance, completed projects, financial matters, and goals for next year.

**Accountability of Office Holders**
Every office holder will be answerable to the Committee for the work given to him, for funds, and for the use of powers.

**Policy for Inactive Members**
A member who does not attend meetings regularly or does not do his duties should first get a written reminder, and then suitable action under the organization's rules should follow.

**Respect for Difference of Opinion**
Every member has the right to give his opinion in meetings. Difference of opinion will not be seen as personal opposition, and the final decision will be accepted according to the fixed procedure.

**Media and Social Media Policy**
Only an authorized spokesperson or appointed office holder will give official statements on media or social media. Unverified information, rumors, and character attacks on any person should be avoided.

**Protecting the Organization from Political and Personal Interests**
If the organization is founded on a non-political basis, then its name, resources, or platform should not be used for the election campaign of any political party, candidate, or person.

**Procedure for Emergencies**
In case of law and order problems, natural disasters, fire, accidents, or any emergency, the role of the organization, contact persons, and the way to contact relevant government departments should be decided in advance.

**Principle for Changing the Manifesto**
A change in the manifesto or basic policy should not be made by one person's decision, but by a proper resolution and the needed majority in a fixed meeting.

### One More Basic Clause

> The organization will not be anyone's personal estate. All office holders, members, and committees of the organization will be bound by this manifesto, the constitution, and approved resolutions. Every power will come with accountability, and every decision will be made according to the principles of common good, transparency, the rule of law, and justice.

# Part 3: Manifesto of the Togh Aman Committee

## Our Resolve

To work for peace, justice, transparency, respect for the law, protection of basic rights, and lasting development in the area. We will work together, as a non-political body, above personal interests.

**1. Establishing Peace and Order**
Promote peace, brotherhood, mutual respect, and tolerance in the area, and play a positive role in solving disputes peacefully.

**2. Preventing Crime and Social Evils**
Cooperate with the relevant departments, by legal and peaceful means, against drug dealing, illegal activities, gambling, immoral activities, and other crimes. Create awareness and promote positive activities to keep young people away from harmful activities.

**3. Protecting the Rights of Children and Women**
Raise a voice for the legal, educational, social, and basic rights of children and women, and play a role in creating a safe and respectful environment for them.

**4. Development of the Area and Public Interest**
Present public problems effectively before the relevant departments about education, health, clean water, roads, electricity, drainage, and other basic facilities, and play a positive role in watching over development projects.

**5. Transparency in Royalty and Development Funds**
Get information about royalty, development funds, and other public resources of the area. Contact the relevant departments legally for their transparent and fair use. Try to make sure the benefit of these resources reaches the truly deserving people.

**6. Protection of Shamilat and Common Lands**
Take legal steps and contact the relevant government departments to stop illegal sale or purchase, occupation, or wrongful use of the area's common lands, and government or shared lands.

**7. Jobs and Sports Opportunities for Youth**
Work to promote jobs, technical training, skills, business opportunities, and sports facilities for young people, so that the young generation gets positive and constructive paths.

**8. Transparency in the Patwari and Revenue System**
Raise a voice, in a legal and institutional way, for transparency in the patwari/revenue system, correct records, timely services to people, and against unnecessary delay or corruption.

**9. Promotion of Education**
Work for quality education in the area, better school facilities, educational help for deserving students, and encouragement of higher and technical education for young people.

**10. Health and Basic Facilities**
Contact the relevant authorities and point out public problems to improve basic health centres, medicines, medical facilities, clean water, cleanliness, and other basic needs.

**11. Merit, Transparency, and Accountability**
Run all matters of the organization on merit, transparency, joint decisions, and written procedure. Never use the organization's platform to give personal benefit to any person, family, or group.

**12. Public Complaints and Legal Guidance**
Collect problems and complaints of the people in an organized way, document them, and pass them on to the relevant government departments, and take legal guidance when needed.

**13. Protecting the Environment and Natural Resources**
Promote awareness and practical steps to protect the area's natural resources, water, pastures, forests, and environment.

**14. Community Unity and Social Service**
Promote unity, brotherhood, and mutual help among different families and groups of the area, and set up a system of community service in natural disasters, emergencies, and other times of need.

### One Important Principle

> The organization will work not for any person, family, political party, or personal benefit, but for the common good of the whole area, the rule of law, justice, transparency, and progress.
