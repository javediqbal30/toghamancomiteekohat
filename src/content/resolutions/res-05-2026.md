### Problem Background
Residents of Togh Bala reported recurrent pipe bursts along the primary gravity-fed water conduit. Previous verbal appeals to municipal authorities remained unaddressed.

### Constitutional Basis
Pursuant to Article 4 (Development Work) and Chapter 7 (Resolution System) of the Togh Aman Committee Constitution, the executive committee formally deliberated on this issue and passed this binding resolution by unanimous vote.

### Action Plan
1. Dispatch written petition signed by the President to the Executive Engineer (PHE), Kohat.
2. Form a 3-member community oversight team to supervise on-site excavation.
3. Ensure zero commercial misappropriation of community water lines.
