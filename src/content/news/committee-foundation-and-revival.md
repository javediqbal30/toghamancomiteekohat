The vision for a united, non-political community platform for Togh began in **July 2024**, when Founder **Hadayatullah Khan** laid the foundation of the committee. 

Recognizing that disputes, drug abuse, and lack of municipal attention were harming the future of young generations, the founder mobilized neighborhood elders, religious scholars, and youth representatives.

In **July 2026**, the committee was systematically reorganized, fortified with a formal 18-chapter Constitution, written rules of procedure, and financial transparency protocols. This revival culminated in the formal public opening on 25 August 2026 under the executive leadership of President **Malik Ihsanullah**.
