In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.

Alhamdulillah! Today, **25 August 2026**, our Togh Aman Committee has been formally opened.

The primary objective of this Committee is to make our village free of all kinds of narcotics (including ice/crystal meth), to keep a close watch on illegal scrap yards and unauthorized activities, and to maintain peace and communal order across Togh Bala and Payan. 

Insha'Allah, we will strictly operate within the boundaries of Pakistan's law, standing shoulder to shoulder with law enforcement agencies and police administration, solving community challenges through mutual consultation and documented consensus.

We sincerely thank all respected elders, residents of the neighborhood, and friends for their steadfast cooperation and attendance at the opening session. Your unity is the guarantee of our village's peace and prosperous future.
