In a special policy session following the formal inauguration, President **Malik Ihsanullah** affirmed the committee's uncompromising resolve to protect the moral and physical safety of Togh Bala & Payan.

### Core Strategic Priorities:
1. **Zero Tolerance Against Drugs:** Active coordination with local police to rid our villages of crystal meth (ice) and narcotics.
2. **Monitoring Illegal Scrap Yards:** Scrap yards and unauthorized scrap collectors will be placed under strict surveillance to eradicate bicycle, cable, and property theft.
3. **Respect for Moral Environment:** Vulgarity and public indecency will be actively discouraged through community consensus and parental awareness.
4. **Absolute Rule of Law:** No member will ever take the law into their own hands. Every grievance is routed through formal legal petitions and administrative forums.
